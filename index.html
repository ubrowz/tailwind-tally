Total width of the neutral band, centered on crosswind (perpendicular to the wind) — e.g. 10° means ±5° either side. Neutral segments count as neither easy nor hard.

Total width of the "very" bands, centered on dead-on tailwind (0°) and dead-on headwind (180°) — e.g. 40° means the closest 20° to either counts as very easy/very hard. Used by the Route map and its calculations below, not the rest of the page.

Advanced

Easy/hard here is a plain distance count: every metre where the wind has any tailwind component at all counts as easy, and any headwind component counts as hard — regardless of how strong that push or drag actually is.
